## 1. Statement classifier: a fifteen-label knowledge-type taxonomy over five coarse types

`create` · knowledge state **authoritative** · status `ready` · candidate `cand-001-r1` v2

**Slug** `statement-classifier-a-fifteen-label-knowledge-type-taxonomy-over-five-coarse-ty`

### Summary

The classifier assigns a knowledge type to one short statement and returns one record: fifteen fine labels each mapping to exactly one of five coarse types by lookup rather than judgment, plus `general` assigned by code when no test fires. It answers what kind of statement something is and never whether it is correct; how established a statement is lives in a separate `status` field, and where it came from in `provenance`. Classifications are appended, never overwritten, so re-running under a new prompt or model adds a record rather than replacing one.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

4. A classification is a pure function of (statement, prompt_version, classifier_model); records are appended and never overwritten.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

5. `general` is never offered to the model as a label. A named escape hatch measured catastrophically: 96.1% agreement with Fleiss κ −0.001 and zero identifications of the minority class.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

6. Permission-shaped statements have no home in the taxonomy and will land in `general` or be pulled into `obligation` by the modal -- a named, unfixed gap.

### Summary

Each of the fifteen labels carries a Cue stating a surface pattern checkable without inferring intent, an Excludes list with at least two non-firing conditions, and two exemplars -- one document-style, one conversational -- which are part of the required shape and are not reproduced in this entry. ## 3. What the statement classifier's in-house measurements found, including four rejected designs

`create` · knowledge state **supported** · status `ready` · candidate `cand-003-r1` v2

**Slug** `what-the-statement-classifier-s-in-house-measurements-found-including-four-rejec`

### Summary

Twenty-two runs on this codebook with no answer key in existence, roughly 10,000 assignments. The definition pass is the single largest effect: fine α 0.844 to 0.934 with eight raters on 160 statements, winning 100% of 400 paired resamples, and it came from domain judgment rather than from the measurements -- sixteen structural designs moved ±0.05 or lost while fourteen rewritten definitions moved +0.090. Four designs were tested and rejected: confidence-driven abstention, the boolean battery resolved by priority, a generality test for `principle`, and the taxonomy restructure whose gain a control attributed to the item set rather than the codebook. All four are instances of one rule the document derives: anything turning one classification decision into two costs more in the second step than it gains in the first.

### Assertions (8)

1. The definition pass moved fine α from 0.844 to 0.934 with eight raters on 160 statements, winning in 100% of 400 paired resamples, 95% CI [0.905, 0.956].

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

2. Self-reported confidence cannot drive the residual bucket: an absolute threshold of 90 collapsed α to 0.605 with 86% of assignments falling through.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

3. The boolean battery resolved by priority measured 0.785 fine against 0.877 for single choice of fifteen; it is retained only for multi-label output, not for agreement.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

4. A generality test written for `principle` was tested and rejected: collisions rose 17 to 19 and `principle` α fell 0.861 to 0.797.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

5. A control holding the item set fixed showed the taxonomy restructure bought +0.009 fine and −0.075 coarse, against a headline of +0.080 and +0.061 -- the gain was the item set.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

6. Asking for status improves type agreement rather than costing it: fine α 0.841 to 0.877 on the same 160 items.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

7. Real published documents cost roughly 0.05 against generated statements: fine α 0.894 on 85 statements drawn from Sharpe (1991), De Bondt & Thaler (1985) and a market note.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

8. A four-rater run cannot distinguish designs -- the same design run twice scored 0.898 and 0.844.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

## 4. Statement classifier evaluation protocol and its pre-registered kill criterion

`create` · knowledge state **authoritative** · status `ready` · candidate `cand-004-r1` v2

**Slug** `statement-classifier-evaluation-protocol-and-its-pre-registered-kill-criterion`

### Summary

Acceptance is agreement with human labels; cross-model agreement is a stability check only, because inter-model agreement is not evidence of correctness -- four models once agreed on 96.1% of labels with Fleiss κ −0.001 while missing 75% of the minority class. The gold set samples document-derived and conversational strata separately, oversamples rare labels, and requires human-human agreement reported first as the ceiling. Metrics are α at both tiers separately plus per-category agreement, never a single aggregate. A kill criterion is pre-registered: run the ablation with labels present versus stripped, user preference does not count as a gain, and if no query class improves the classifier is decoration -- keep temporal validity and drop the rest.

### Assertions (7)

1. The acceptance test is agreement with human labels; inter-model agreement is a stability check only and is not evidence of correctness.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

2. The gold set samples both strata separately, oversamples rare fine labels, and reports human-human agreement first as the ceiling.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

3. Metrics are Krippendorff's α at fine and coarse tiers separately, plus per-category agreement, the `general` share, the `multi_fire` rate and per-model divergence.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

4. The strongest direct evidence about typed information is negative and human: two controlled experiments found no effect on task performance (n=65 and n=76).

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

5. No source in the reviewed evidence isolates type labels from anything else; the counterfactual of the same corpus and retriever with labels stripped has never been run. The one payoff replicating across three agent-memory systems is temporal validity.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

6. Pre-registered kill criterion: run the ablation, user preference does not count as a gain, and if no query class improves, keep temporal validity and drop the rest.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

7. Whether the coarse tier is genuinely more reliable than the fine tier remains unestablished in the literature; until measured on the gold set the two-tier design is a bet.

   *backed by* `asmt-0001`, `asmt-0002`, `asmt-0003`

`evaluation`, `ablation`, `gold standard`, `pre-registration`

## 5. Statement classifier contract: what it promises, what it refuses, and where it knows it falls short

`create` · knowledge state **authoritative** · status `ready` · candidate `cand-005-r1` v2

**Slug** `statement-classifier-contract-what-it-promises-what-it-refuses-and-where-it-know`

### Summary

The interface is one statement in, one classification record out, with optional context used to classify that statement but never to reclassify its neighbours. The classifier refuses two things by design: it does not judge whether a statement is correct, and it produces no relationships between statements -- relations such as resolved_by and opposes are named as the next layer and are out of scope for v1. Confidence is elicited internally but never published; uncertainty is expressed structurally instead, with no test firing meaning abstention and two or more meaning ambiguity. The document labels every claim by what backs it, and records the gaps it cannot close -- permission-shaped statements have no home, and splitting a multi-statement message is out of scope.

### Assertions (6)

1. The contract is one statement in, one record out; context classifies that statement and never reclassifies the neighbours.

   *backed by* `asmt-0004`

2. The classifier does not judge truth and produces no relationships between statements; relations are deliberately out of scope for v1.

   *backed by* `asmt-0004`

3. No confidence score is published. Uncertainty is structural: tests_fired == 0 is abstention, tests_fired >= 2 is ambiguity.

   *backed by* `asmt-0004`

4. Claims are labelled [VERIFIED], [MEASURED] or [DESIGN], and nothing is presented as evidence-backed unless a number is attached.

   *backed by* `asmt-0004`

5. Anything that turns one classification decision into two costs more in the second step than it gains in the first -- derived from three separate in-house measurements.

   *backed by* `asmt-0004`

6. Known gaps are recorded rather than hidden: permission-shaped statements have no home, splitting multi-statement messages is out of scope, and no evidence was found on how much context a conversational statement needs.

   *backed by* `asmt-0004`
