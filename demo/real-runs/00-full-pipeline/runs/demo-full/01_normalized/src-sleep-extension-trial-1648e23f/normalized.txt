# Sleep Extension and Delayed Recall: A Randomized Trial

## Design

The trial randomized 42 healthy adults to a nine-hour sleep opportunity or to their habitual schedule over four weeks.
Participants completed a delayed word-recall task at baseline and again at week four.

## Results

Delayed word recall improved 8.2% in the extension group versus 1.1% in controls.
Total sleep time rose by 47 minutes per night in the extension group.

## Limitations

The trial was not blinded, and follow-up lasted only four weeks, so durability beyond one month is unknown.

## Open questions

Whether the same effect holds in adults over sixty-five remains untested.
